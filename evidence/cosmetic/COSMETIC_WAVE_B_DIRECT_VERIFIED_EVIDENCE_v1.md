Market: United States

## Evidence rules
1. Preserve procedure, material/method, priced unit and price type.
2. Do not combine veneer types into a site-created universal range.
3. Dental bonding is priced per tooth in the CareCredit procedure cost list; do not convert it into a per-mouth estimate.
4. Professional whitening methods remain separate. Laser and Zoom/UV rows must not be merged into one invented range.
5. Published prices are reference context, never calculator defaults or personal quotes.
6. No procedure/material recommendation, candidacy decision or diagnosis.
7. No universal insurance percentage or guaranteed cosmetic benefit.

## COS-001 — CareCredit/Synchrony dental veneers article
Source: https://www.carecredit.com/well-u/health-wellness/dental-veneers-cost-dental-veneers-financing/
Observed article date: 2026-08-14.
Verified published U.S. rows:
- Single composite veneer, lab manufactured: $1,068 average; $783–$1,760 range.
- Single composite veneer, dental office: $751 average; $541–$1,220 range.
- Single porcelain veneer: $1,455 average; $1,020–$2,506 range.
- Single porcelain laminate veneer: $1,781 average; $1,243–$2,997 range.
- Full set, described by the article as six to eight single veneers: $13,954 average; $9,712–$24,046 range.
Scope notes: article says consultation and potential maintenance/repair are not included in the type table. Keep the full-set row separate from single-veneer rows.

## COS-002 — ADA dental glossary
Source: https://www.ada.org/publications/cdt/glossary-dental-terms
Use: terminology only. ADA defines a laminate veneer as a thin covering of the facial surface of a tooth, usually tooth-colored. Do not use this source to recommend veneers or determine candidacy.

## COS-004 — CareCredit/Synchrony professional whitening article
Source: https://www.carecredit.com/well-u/health-wellness/teeth-whitening-costs/
Article date: 2024-11-01.
Research: 2024 Synchrony Average Procedural Cost Study for the published professional-method rows; methodology states 50 states and District of Columbia.
Verified:
- Dental whitening by laser: $792 average; $611–$1,368 range.
- Dental whitening by Zoom!: $583 average; $463–$1,011 range.
The article also lists dentist-provided take-home kits at $375–$500, but that row is attributed to a separate provider source rather than the Synchrony national cost study. First-version DEN-029 should therefore lead with the laser and Zoom rows and not present the take-home-kit range as the same national dataset.

## COS-005 — ADA whitening overview

Use: terminology/category boundaries. ADA distinguishes dentist-supervised at-home and in-office approaches and describes peroxide-based bleaching. Do not turn this clinical overview into a treatment recommendation.
