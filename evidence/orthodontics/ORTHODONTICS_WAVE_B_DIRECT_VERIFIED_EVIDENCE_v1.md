Verified: 2026-09-17
Market: United States
Pages: DEN-017 `/invisalign-cost-calculator/`, DEN-020 `/braces-cost/`, DEN-027 `/dental-retainer-cost/`
Registry: DEN-017 → CALC-017; DEN-020 → CALC-020; DEN-027 → no calculator.

## Evidence rules
1. Preserve treatment type. Clear-aligner evidence must not be silently relabeled as Invisalign-only evidence.
2. Preserve price unit and price type. Average-by-type rows are not one combined braces range.
3. Do not use a published national reference as a calculator default or as the reader's quote.
4. Do not recommend aligners, braces, a brace type or a retainer type. Treatment suitability belongs to an orthodontic professional.
5. Retainer inclusion in an orthodontic package is unknown unless the written quote states it.
6. Do not assume a universal orthodontic insurance percentage, lifetime maximum, age rule or coverage amount.
7. Replacement-retainer pricing is provider-specific unless a national source clearly establishes otherwise.

## ORTH-001 — CareCredit/Synchrony clear-aligner article
Source: https://www.carecredit.com/well-u/health-wellness/invisalign-cost-invisalign-financing/
Article date observed: 2025-03-28
Research: ASQ360° research across the 50 U.S. states and District of Columbia, conducted in 2023 on behalf of Synchrony/CareCredit.
Verified price scope:
- Clear aligners: $5,108 national average.
- Clear aligners: $1,800–$8,100 range.
Critical scope restriction: the article title references Invisalign®, but the priced category is **clear aligners**. The source itself describes “clear aligners” as the generic term and Invisalign as a well-known brand. Therefore DEN-017 may answer the Invisalign query, but must label the published benchmark as clear-aligner evidence rather than an Invisalign-only national benchmark.
Publication-safe wording: “A current U.S. CareCredit/Synchrony study reports a $5,108 average and $1,800–$8,100 range for clear aligner treatment. The study prices clear aligners as a category, so these figures should not be presented as an Invisalign-only national price.”
Blocked: any claim that $5,108 or $1,800–$8,100 is proven to be Invisalign-only.

## ORTH-003 — CareCredit braces article
Source: https://www.carecredit.com/well-u/health-wellness/dental-braces-cost-dental-braces-financing/
Article date observed: 2025-07-19.
Verified type-specific price rows:
- Metal braces: $6,343 average; $2,500–$10,000 range.
- Ceramic braces: $5,834 average; $4,480–$11,312 range.
- Lingual braces: $9,221 average; $7,321–$17,411 range.
- Clear aligners: $5,108 average; $1,800–$8,100 range, but clear aligners belong primarily to DEN-017 and are comparison-only on DEN-020.
The article also describes state averages for metal braces and says those state prices exclude accessory gear and potential insurance coverage.
Page rule for DEN-020: lead with fixed-brace type evidence rather than manufacturing one overall range from different treatment categories.

Sources:

- Treatment selection is individualized; the site must not decide which appliance is appropriate for a reader.

## ORTH-005 — American Association of Orthodontists retainer guidance
Sources:
- https://aaoinfo.org/treatments/retainers/
- https://aaoinfo.org/resources/faqs/page/13/
Use: retainer terminology and replacement-price limitation.
Verified distinctions:

Do not convert the CareCredit new-retainer averages into a guaranteed replacement-retainer price.
