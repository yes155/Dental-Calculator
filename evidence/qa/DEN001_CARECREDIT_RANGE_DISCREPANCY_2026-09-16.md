## Observed inconsistency

On 2026-09-16, the live CareCredit general implant guide showed two different upper bounds for the same single-tooth implant benchmark:

- page summary / teaser: `$1,646–$4,175`
- body under `How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?`: `$1,646–$4,157`

The dedicated CareCredit `Single Tooth Dental Implants Cost and Procedure Guide` also reports `$1,646–$4,157` with a `$2,143` national average.

## Editorial decision

For DEN-001, use the dedicated single-tooth page as the controlling price source:

- national average: `$2,143`
- range: `$1,646–$4,157`

Use the general implant guide for its explicit scope statement that the benchmark covers the process/material for implanting the artificial root and excludes the crown, and that state values also exclude extraction and office-related fees.

Do not silently substitute `$4,175` into DEN-001 while the publisher's own pages are internally inconsistent.
