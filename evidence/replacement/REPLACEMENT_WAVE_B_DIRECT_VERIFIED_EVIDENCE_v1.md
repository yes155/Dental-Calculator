Registry: DEN-024 → CALC-024; DEN-030 → CALC-030.

## Evidence rules
1. Preserve prosthesis type, priced unit and price type.
2. Published national references are context only; they never prefill calculators or become a reader's quote.
3. Bridge type labels must not automatically alter a user-entered quote.
4. Denture categories and units stay separate. In particular, an implant-supported **per-implant** figure must not be multiplied into a full denture total unless the user's own written quote supplies the total.
5. Do not manufacture one universal bridge range or one universal denture range by combining unlike categories.
6. Do not recommend a bridge/denture type, determine candidacy, diagnose missing-tooth needs or decide whether implants are appropriate.
7. No universal dental-insurance percentage, deductible, annual maximum or prosthodontic benefit assumption.

## REP-003 — ADA dental glossary
Source: https://www.ada.org/publications/cdt/glossary-dental-terms
Use: terminology only.
Verified:
- A fixed partial denture is a prosthetic replacement of one or more missing teeth attached to abutment natural teeth or implant replacements.
- A pontic is an artificial tooth on a fixed partial denture (bridge).
- A Maryland bridge is a resin-bonded fixed partial denture with wing-like retainers bonded to abutment teeth.
No treatment recommendation or candidacy inference.

## REP-005 — ADA denture terminology
Sources:
- https://www.ada.org/publications/cdt/glossary-dental-terms
- https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/dentures
Use: terminology/category boundaries only.
Verified:
- Complete denture replaces the full dentition of an upper or lower arch.
- Removable partial denture replaces one or more missing teeth and can be removed by the patient.
- Dentures can be nonimplant-retained or implant-retained.
No selection recommendation.
