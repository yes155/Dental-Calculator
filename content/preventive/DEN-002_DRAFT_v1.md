# Dental cleaning cost

Delta Dental reports that a **standard dental cleaning costs about $85–$160 without dental benefits**. That figure is for a standard cleaning, not a deep cleaning, periodontal maintenance visit, or a broader exam/cleaning/X-ray package. Your written estimate should show whether exam, X-rays, fluoride or other items are included or charged separately.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah.  
Evidence checked September 17, 2026.

## How much does a dental cleaning cost?

Delta Dental currently reports an average cost of **$85–$160 for a standard dental cleaning without dental benefits**. Its consumer page does not disclose the exact static-range sample or data date, so this is a current publisher-reported benchmark rather than an independently verified national fee survey.

Cigna reports an adult cleaning average of roughly **$104**, citing the ADA Health Policy Institute's 2022 Survey of Dental Fees. Humana reports **$80–$109 without insurance for an adult routine cleaning in the Orlando, Florida area**. Keep that Humana figure local; do not generalize it nationally.

## Dental cleaning cost calculator

Use CALC-002 with a written cleaning estimate. Enter either one total or separate charges, mark whether exam, X-rays, fluoride and any other item are included, separate, not listed or unclear, and add an insurer estimate only if you already have one for the same quote.

The published $85–$160 reference never fills in the calculator or changes its arithmetic.

## What does a standard dental cleaning price include?

A quoted "standard cleaning" amount does not prove that every part of the visit is bundled. The controlling question is what the written estimate actually says.

If the provider lists one combined total, record whether exam, imaging, fluoride or another charge is included. If the provider lists separate charges, enter those separately rather than subtracting unrelated benchmark prices to invent a cleaning-only amount.

## Are the exam and X-rays included in a cleaning quote?

They may be included, separate, or absent from a particular written estimate. Do not assume one arrangement applies to every office.

CareCredit publishes a **$203** observation labeled as a dental exam with cleaning and X-rays. That is a broader bundle and is **not** used on this page as a standalone cleaning price. X-ray type-level costs belong on the dental X-ray cost page; exam-led pricing belongs on the dentist-visit page.

## What can change a dental cleaning quote?

Published prices vary by source, geography and scope. The current evidence supports a broad Delta Dental standard-cleaning benchmark, an older Cigna/ADA adult average, and a Humana Orlando example. It does not support a universal formula that adjusts a cleaning quote by ZIP code, age, visit frequency or symptoms.

The written quote may also differ because exam, imaging, fluoride or other services are included or billed separately.

## How insurance can change what you pay

Dental-plan rules vary. Some plans may cover preventive services differently from other services, but there is no universal percentage or guaranteed $0 cleaning visit.

CALC-002 does not calculate deductibles, coinsurance, network allowed amounts, annual maximums or frequency limits. It subtracts only a same-quote insurer estimate that the user enters directly.

## Routine cleaning, deep cleaning and periodontal maintenance are different

This page owns standard/routine cleaning cost. Scaling and root planing, often called deep cleaning, has a different pricing unit and belongs on the deep-teeth-cleaning page. Ongoing periodontal maintenance has its own visit-level pricing page.

The calculator does not decide which service a reader needs and does not turn symptoms into a treatment category.
