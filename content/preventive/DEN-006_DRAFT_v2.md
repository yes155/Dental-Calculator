# Deep teeth cleaning cost by quadrant

Delta Dental reports an average cost of **$180–$295 without dental benefits for scaling and root planing for one quadrant**, or one-fourth of the mouth. That is a per-quadrant benchmark, not a default full-mouth total. The number of quadrants on an individual treatment estimate must come from the written quote; this page does not decide how much treatment a reader needs.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah.  
Evidence checked September 17, 2026.

## How much does deep teeth cleaning cost?

Delta Dental reports **$180–$295 for scaling and root planing for one quadrant** without dental benefits. The source explains a quadrant as one-fourth of the mouth. Its consumer page does not disclose the exact static-range data date or sample methodology, so treat the figure as a current publisher-reported benchmark rather than a personalized quote.

Humana gives a narrower local example of **$235–$303 per quadrant in Orlando, Florida**. Keep that figure local. Do not merge it with the Delta range to manufacture a national range, and do not automatically multiply either observation by four.

## What does “per quadrant” mean in a deep cleaning quote?

A quadrant is one-fourth of the mouth. A provider may quote scaling and root planing for one or more quadrants, but this page does not infer that number from symptoms, price or a published average.

CALC-006 calculates an average per quoted quadrant only after you enter and confirm the quadrant count from the written estimate. If the total does not divide evenly, the result is labeled as an approximate arithmetic average from that quote.

## What can be included or charged separately?

ADA MouthHealthy describes scaling and root planing as a deep cleaning below the gumline and notes that it may take more than one visit and may involve local anesthetic. That does not establish what a specific provider's price includes.

Humana lists factors such as the teeth treated, location, provider, X-rays and anesthesia when discussing cost. Its price statement does not establish that X-rays or anesthesia are included. Record these items from the written estimate as included, a separate charge, not listed or not sure rather than assuming a standard package.

## What changes a scaling and root planing quote?

The current evidence supports keeping the quoted treatment extent visible. Humana also identifies factors such as the treated teeth, location and provider. The available sources do not support a universal formula for adjusting a quote by those factors.

When comparing two estimates, confirm that they cover the same stated number of quadrants and ask whether anesthesia, imaging or other items are included or separate.

## Routine cleaning, deep cleaning, debridement and maintenance are not the same

This page owns scaling-and-root-planing pricing. Standard routine cleaning belongs on the dental-cleaning page, and ongoing periodontal-maintenance pricing belongs on its own page. Do not treat gross debridement, laser procedures or unspecified “gum cleaning” labels as interchangeable with SRP unless the written quote clearly identifies the service.

These distinctions are for quote comparison only. This page does not determine which service a reader needs or what follow-up care is appropriate.

## How insurance can affect the patient amount

Plan rules vary. Deductibles, coinsurance, network rules, frequency limits, annual maximums and the exact covered service may affect the patient amount under a particular policy, but this page does not assume any of those values.

CALC-006 subtracts only a user-entered insurer estimate confirmed to apply to the same written quote. If insurance is unknown, it leaves the after-insurance amount unknown.
