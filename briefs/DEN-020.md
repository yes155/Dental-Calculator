## Direct-answer target
“Current U.S. cost research varies by brace type. CareCredit/Synchrony reports metal braces at $6,343 average ($2,500–$10,000), ceramic braces at $5,834 average ($4,480–$11,312), and lingual braces at $9,221 average ($7,321–$17,411). These are separate treatment categories, not one combined braces price range.”
