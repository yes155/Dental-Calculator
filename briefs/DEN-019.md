## Direct answer
Current CareCredit/Synchrony data reports single composite office-made veneers at $751 average ($541–$1,220), lab-made composite at $1,068 ($783–$1,760), single porcelain at $1,455 ($1,020–$2,506), porcelain laminate at $1,781 ($1,243–$2,997), and a six-to-eight-veneer full set at $13,954 ($9,712–$24,046).
