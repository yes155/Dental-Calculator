## 5. Direct-answer target

Open with the evidence limitation and useful number, not the publisher name:

**Current U.S. cost research does not provide a clean inlay-only national range. The strongest recent study reports a $976 average and a $755–$1,774 range for a single ceramic inlay or onlay combined. Because the study groups the two restorations together, those figures should not be presented as an inlay-only national price range.**

Immediately after the answer, attribute the benchmark to CareCredit/Synchrony and state the 2024 study / 50-states-plus-DC scope. Explain that an individual written inlay quote still needs its material, surface count and inclusion status checked.

Editorial attribution rule: **answer first, source second**. Do not rotate publishers for cosmetic variety.
