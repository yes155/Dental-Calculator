## 5. Direct-answer target

Published crown prices vary by material. CareCredit reports, for example, a **$1,399 average and $915–$3,254 range for porcelain crowns**, a **$1,114 average and $770–$2,454 range for porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns**, and a **$1,211 average and $821–$2,861 range for metallic crowns**. These material categories should remain separate. The headline `$697–$1,399` comparison of average prices is not the full observed crown-price range.

## 8. Calculator role — CALC-022
Purpose: organize a written crown quote by confirmed crown count and quoted material/type while keeping separately quoted build-up/foundation and other charges explicit.  
Inputs: one-total vs itemized crown charges; crown count; material/type from quote if stated; build-up/foundation status and optional amount; exam/imaging/other adjunct statuses/amounts; optional same-quote insurer estimate.  
Outputs: entered quote total; optional average per crown; quote-scope summary; conditional patient amount.  
Published material ranges: reference-only; never auto-fill arithmetic.

Prohibited: selecting crown material; deciding a crown is needed; treating repair/recement as new-crown pricing; inventing build-up or replacement-crown defaults; importing implant-supported crown prices; applying default insurance percentages.

Implementation: `data/calculator-specs/CALC-022.md`, `src/assets/crown-calculator-core.mjs`, `src/assets/crown-calculator-ui.mjs`, and `/dental-crown-cost/`. Automated regression/build QA passed at implementation head `ce21954fcb516d6ffde743e1498c3961f4948ceb`.
