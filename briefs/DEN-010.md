## 5. Direct-answer target

Published filling prices vary substantially by material. CareCredit reports separate U.S. ranges, including **$108–$256 for silver amalgam** and **$173–$439 for composite resin**. Do not treat the lowest value from one material and the highest value from another as a single standard-filling range.

## 8. Calculator role — CALC-010
Purpose: organize a written filling quote by restoration count and the material/surface/location labels actually shown.  
Inputs: one-total vs itemized restoration charges; restoration count; material from quote if stated; optional surface count/location labels; separately quoted adjunct statuses/amounts; optional same-quote insurer estimate.  
Outputs: entered quote total; optional average per entered restoration; itemized scope summary; conditional patient amount.  
Published material ranges: reference-only; never auto-fill arithmetic.

Prohibited: selecting filling material; diagnosing cavity depth; deriving surfaces from symptoms; treating porcelain inlay/onlay as DEN-010 content; inventing coverage percentage.
