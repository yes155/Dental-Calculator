Page entity: dental bridge cost.
User task: compare current U.S. bridge prices by named bridge type and estimate the patient portion of a written bridge quote without converting published averages into a personal treatment quote.

## Direct answer
Current CareCredit/Synchrony evidence reports a traditional single-tooth bridge at $5,197 average ($4,100–$9,650), cantilever at $1,041 average ($800–$2,030), Maryland at $1,569 average ($1,200–$2,910), and implant-supported at $5,195 average ($4,000–$9,450). These are separate bridge categories, not one combined bridge range.
