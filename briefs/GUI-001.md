## 6. Worked-example freeze
Baseline assumptions:
- billed charge $1,000
- allowed amount $800
- provider waives $200 fee difference
- remaining deductible $50
- patient coinsurance 20%
- insurer share under the hypothetical setup 80%
- remaining insurer benefit $1,000
- no copay, exclusion, waiting/frequency denial, secondary insurer or prior patient payment

Baseline result: insurer $600; patient $200; $200 waived; reconciliation $1,000.
Variation A: remaining benefit $300 → insurer $300; patient $500.
Variation B: $200 fee difference becomes payable → insurer $600; patient $400.

These values are not procedure prices, national averages, FEDVIP benefit terms or calculator defaults.
