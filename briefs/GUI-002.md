## 6. Main heading vector

### H2 — Where can I look for lower-cost dental care?
- Summarize the verified pathways.

### H2 — Dental and dental-hygiene school clinics
- Explain reduced-cost/supervised model conditionally.
- Require direct verification of public clinic, service scope, fee and wait time.

### H2 — Federally funded health centers and sliding fees
- Explain that health centers may offer dental services.
- HRSA sliding-fee eligibility is based on income and family size.
- Never publish a universal discount amount.

### H2 — Medicaid and CHIP dental coverage
- Children: required dental benefits within program rules.
- Adults: Medicaid dental benefits vary by state.
- No eligibility determination.

### H2 — State, local and community resources
- Health departments, 2-1-1 and similar community-navigation resources.
- No guaranteed program availability.

### H2 — What to verify before booking lower-cost care
- Service offered
- current fee or sliding-fee method
- required eligibility documents
- included/separate exam/X-ray/lab/follow-up charges
- wait list/referral
- insurance/network status if relevant

### H2 — Compare the written quote, not just the advertised discount
- Bridge to owning procedure pages.
- Explain that a percentage discount without the underlying fee and scope is insufficient for comparison.

### H2 — What this guide does not promise
- No clinic availability guarantee, eligibility decision, free-care promise or treatment recommendation.

### H2 — Related dental cost guides
- GUI-001 plus representative procedure owners.
