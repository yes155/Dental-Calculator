## Direct-answer target
“Current U.S. CareCredit/Synchrony cost data reports average prices of $344 for a bonded retainer, $181 for a clear plastic retainer and $302 for a molded acrylic retainer. Those are separate retainer categories; they should not be averaged into one universal retainer price, and replacement fees can be set by the orthodontist.”
