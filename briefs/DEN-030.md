## Direct answer
Current CareCredit/Synchrony evidence reports separate averages such as $2,022 for full traditional removable dentures, $1,784 for partial resin-based dentures, $2,293 for partial metal dentures, and $4,093 per implant for full implant-supported dentures. Categories and units must remain separate.
