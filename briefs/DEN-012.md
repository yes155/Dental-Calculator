## 5. Direct-answer target

Opening may state that CareCredit reports a **$15,176 national average** and **$11,640–$27,500 range** for All-on-4 dental implants, based on 2024 research across all 50 states + DC. The same article describes All-on-4 as replacing teeth in an **upper or lower jaw**, but its price sentence does not independently label the figure “per arch,” so readers must confirm whether their own quote covers one arch or both arches.

Do not double the CareCredit figure to manufacture a full-mouth estimate.
