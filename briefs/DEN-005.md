## 8. Calculator role — CALC-005
Purpose: organize a written root-canal quote and keep the root-canal fee separate from any quoted final restoration/add-ons.  
Inputs: quote mode; tooth category from written quote if stated; root-canal amount; restoration status and optional separate amount; build-up/post/other quoted item status and amounts; optional same-quote insurer estimate.  
Outputs: entered quote total; root-canal base amount; component summary; conditional patient amount after user-entered insurer estimate.  
Reference prices: visible separately; never auto-fill calculator inputs.

Prohibited: diagnosing need for root canal; deciding tooth category from symptoms; assuming a crown is always needed or included; inventing retreatment or specialist premiums; applying a default insurance percentage.

Implementation: `data/calculator-specs/CALC-005.md`, `src/assets/root-canal-calculator-core.mjs`, `src/assets/root-canal-calculator-ui.mjs`, and `/root-canal-cost/`.
