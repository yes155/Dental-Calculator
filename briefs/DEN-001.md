## 5. Direct-answer target

Opening answer may state:

CareCredit reports a national average of **$2,143** for a full-size single-tooth dental implant, with a reported range of **$1,646 to $4,157**. Its guide says this covers the process/material for placing the artificial root and **does not include the dental crown**; state-level figures also leave out extraction and some office-related fees.

Do not merge the Humana Orlando range or the Forbes $3,000–$4,500 secondary benchmark into a synthetic national range.

## 7. Calculator role — CALC-001
Purpose: Total and normalize a user-entered single-tooth or multi-tooth implant quote.  
Default philosophy: quote-input first; no hidden treatment assumptions.  
Reference data: CareCredit implant/artificial-root benchmark may be displayed as a labeled reference, not silently inserted as a user's quote.  
Required inputs: tooth count; quote mode; written total or itemized amounts; core component status/amounts where applicable; insurance mode and verified same-quote insurer estimate if known.  
Outputs: entered total, visible missing/unknown items, optional per-tooth normalization, conditional amount after entered insurance estimate.  
Prohibited: candidacy, treatment selection, inferred graft need, default insurance percentage, guaranteed quote.

### CALC-001 representative UX contract
- Place after the direct-answer cost section, not above the answer.
- Use three guided stages: `Your quote` → `What's included` → `Insurance & result`.
- Keep arithmetic and data assumptions identical to the frozen calculator spec.
- Prefer everyday questions over system terminology: `Does your quote show one total or separate charges?`, `What does this amount cover?`, `Did your insurance company give you an estimated payment for this quote?`.
- Define unavoidable dental terms with an everyday label, e.g. `Connector (abutment)` and `Tooth removal (extraction)`.
- Use compact inputs whose width matches expected data where practical.
- Replace repetitive status dropdowns with consistent choices: Included / Separate charge / Not listed / Not sure.
- Reveal a separate-charge amount only when Separate charge is selected.
- Require status choices for the three core components only; keep secondary items collapsed and initially `Not sure` so uncertainty is not converted into a false assumption.
- Provide `Mark all as Not listed` only as an explicit user action when the written quote contains none of the secondary items.
- If quote mode is itemized, skip the bundle-component stage because itemized lines already define the priced scope in the current frozen formula.
- Use plain result labels such as `Total from your quote` and `Cost per tooth from this quote`; avoid terms such as normalization, scope, or patient responsibility in the primary UI.
- Keep published reference values visually separate from calculator arithmetic.
- Treat a money input and its `$` prefix as one visual validation control.
- Preserve keyboard operation, focus management, error summary, touch target sizing, reduced-motion support and no-storage/no-network behavior.

## 8. Clinical / insurance boundary
