## 5. Direct-answer target

Opening answer may state:

CareCredit reports a national average of **$2,143** for a single-tooth dental implant, with a reported range of **$1,646 to $4,157**. That figure covers the process/material for implanting the artificial root and **does not include the dental crown**; extraction and office-related fees are also excluded from the source's state-level implant figures.

Do not merge the Humana Orlando range or the Forbes $3,000–$4,500 secondary benchmark into a synthetic national range.

## 7. Calculator role — CALC-001
Purpose: Total and normalize a user-entered single-tooth or multi-tooth implant quote.  
Default philosophy: quote-input first; no hidden treatment assumptions.  
Reference data: CareCredit implant-body/process benchmark may be displayed as a labeled reference, not silently inserted as a user's quote.  
Required inputs: tooth count; quote mode; written bundle or itemized amounts; component status/amounts where applicable; insurance mode and verified same-scope insurer estimate if known.  
Outputs: entered subtotal, visible missing/unknown components, optional per-tooth normalization, conditional patient-responsibility estimate.  
Prohibited: candidacy, treatment selection, inferred graft need, default insurance percentage, guaranteed quote.

### CALC-001 representative UX contract
- Place after the direct-answer cost section, not above the answer.
- Use three guided stages: `Your quote` → `Included items` → `Insurance & result`.
- Keep arithmetic and data assumptions identical to the frozen calculator spec.
- Use compact inputs whose width matches expected data where practical.
- Replace repetitive status dropdowns with consistent explicit choices: Included / Separate fee / Not listed / Not sure.
- Reveal a separate-fee amount only when Separate fee is selected.
- Keep core components visible; group less-common adjuncts under progressive disclosure.
- If quote mode is itemized, skip the bundle-component stage because itemized lines already define the priced scope in the current frozen formula.
- Make the result summarize quote total, per-tooth normalization, patient estimate, quote type, tooth count and insurance state.
- Keep published reference values visually separate from calculator arithmetic.
- Preserve keyboard operation, focus management, error summary, touch target sizing, reduced-motion support and no-storage/no-network behavior.

## 8. Clinical / insurance boundary
- FDA/AAP terminology may explain components only.
- Do not recommend titanium vs zirconia, immediate loading, grafting or any implant system.
- Coverage varies by plan; deductible, annual maximum, waiting period, exclusions and network rules belong primarily to GUI-001.
- If insurance contribution is unknown, patient responsibility must remain unknown rather than guessed.
