Dental bonding cost

Current CareCredit/Synchrony U.S. cost data reports a $431 average and $288–$915 range for dental bonding. Its dental procedure cost list labels dental bonding per tooth, so the benchmark should not be converted into a multi-tooth total unless a written quote gives the number of teeth and provider pricing.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 17, 2026

How much does dental bonding cost?

The strongest current national benchmark for this page is $431 average and $288–$915 per tooth. It comes from the 2024 Synchrony Average Procedural Cost Study conducted by ASQ360° across the 50 states and District of Columbia.

Dental bonding cost per tooth

Bonding is commonly priced by the tooth in the current CareCredit cost list. If more than one tooth appears on a quote, use the dental office’s written total or per-tooth amounts rather than multiplying the national benchmark into an expected bill.

What should a bonding quote identify?

Confirm the number of teeth, the amount per tooth or total quoted amount, and any related services the office says are included or separate. An unlisted service is not automatically a $0 charge.

Are exams, cleaning or whitening included?

Not universally. The CareCredit bonding article lists exam/cleaning and professional whitening as possible additional cost considerations, but those rows are separate services. This page does not add them automatically to the bonding benchmark.

What can change a bonding quote?

The published source notes geography, provider and the number of teeth as cost variables. A provider may also price a more involved procedure differently. This page does not diagnose a tooth or determine whether bonding is appropriate.

How insurance can affect dental bonding cost

Coverage depends on the specific plan and the reason/service coding involved. Do not assume a universal reimbursement percentage or cosmetic benefit. Verify the written estimate and plan terms directly.

Dental bonding cost versus veneers

Bonding and veneers are separate procedures with different price units. See the dental veneers cost guide and calculator for veneer-specific type and full-set evidence.
