Methodology

Cost Data Methodology

A dental price is useful only when its procedure, unit, geography, date, price type and inclusion scope are understood. Dental Calculator keeps those attributes attached to the number.

Source priority

We prefer U.S. government and public-health sources, professional dental organizations, peer-reviewed research, universities and dental schools, major insurers and recognized cost datasets. Specialist or dental-practice sources can be used for bounded examples but are not promoted to national benchmarks without supporting methodology.

What we record for an important price

  • procedure or priced entity;
  • geographic scope;
  • date or research vintage;
  • priced unit, such as per tooth, quadrant, arch, visit or named bundle;
  • price type, such as published procedural observation, provider fee, plan copay or allowed amount;
  • known inclusions, exclusions and assumptions;
  • source and whether the value is published, derived or modeled.

We do not manufacture ranges

Dental Calculator does not merge unrelated local fees, plan copays or differently scoped sources merely to create a national range. A bundled price stays a bundled price. A per-quadrant price is not multiplied into a default full-mouth total unless the evidence and user inputs support that calculation.

Local and insurance examples

A clinic or city-specific fee remains local. A named insurance-plan copay remains a plan-specific member amount. Neither becomes a U.S.-wide procedure price just because it is a convenient number.

Evidence gaps

When the available sources do not support a publication claim, the internal workflow uses [SOURCE NEEDED BEFORE PUBLICATION] rather than inventing a value. A page may instead publish a transparent limitation and explain how to interpret a written quote.

Freshness

High-change price and insurance evidence is reviewed on its assigned schedule, commonly quarterly, and when a material source changes or a correction is received. The page’s evidence-check date identifies the review point; it is not a price guarantee.
