How much does wisdom teeth removal cost?

The most useful national reference is the row that matches the written quote's tooth count and wording. Do not multiply the single-tooth benchmark to predict a four-tooth bill, and do not relabel an impacted-tooth price as a routine extraction.

Published wisdom-tooth prices by quoted unit

Published categoryPublished amount
Wisdom tooth extraction — single tooth$363 average
Wisdom tooth removal — single impacted tooth$835 average
Wisdom tooth extraction — four teeth$2,685
Wisdom teeth removal — four impacted teeth$3,340

Source: CareCredit dental procedure cost list. The rows keep their original tooth-count and impaction scope.

What may be separate in a wisdom-tooth quote?

Imaging, sedation or anesthesia, graft services and other adjuncts may be included, separately billed or not listed. The calculator keeps those states explicit and never converts an unknown item to $0.

Why impacted and non-impacted price labels must stay separate

CareCredit publishes separate extraction categories, including impacted-tooth categories. Those labels describe source categories, not a diagnosis for a reader. A dental professional determines the actual procedure shown on the treatment plan.

How insurance can affect what you pay

Coverage is plan-specific. This calculator has no default percentage, deductible or oral-surgery benefit. Enter an insurer estimate only when it covers the same teeth and quote lines.

Wisdom teeth removal versus other tooth extractions

Wisdom-tooth pricing belongs on this page. For non-wisdom extraction categories, use the tooth extraction cost guide and calculator.
