What should a bridge quote identify?

Confirm the bridge type, the total written amount, the teeth or prosthetic unit covered by that amount, and any separate services or components listed by the dental office.

Are crowns, pontics or other services included?

Not universally. A bridge can involve a pontic and supporting retainers or crowns, and implant-supported work may involve additional implant-related services. The calculator does not assume any unlisted component is included or costs $0.

What can change a dental bridge quote?

Bridge type, material, number of prosthetic units, provider, geography and included services can affect a written quote. This page does not choose a bridge design or determine whether a bridge is suitable for a reader.

How insurance can affect what you pay

Dental benefits are plan-specific. There is no universal bridge coverage percentage, deductible or annual maximum built into this calculator. Enter only an insurer estimate that covers the same scope as the written quote.

Dental bridge cost versus dentures and implants

Dentures, bridges and implant procedures use different priced units. See dentures cost and the dental implant cost calculator rather than mixing those benchmarks into the bridge total.
