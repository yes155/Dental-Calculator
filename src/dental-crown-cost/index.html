How much does a dental crown cost?

Published U.S. crown prices vary substantially by material. See the table below for the material-specific averages and ranges from CareCredit's U.S. cost research.

Average prices across crown types should not be treated as one generic crown-price range. Keep each material label attached to its own average and reported range.

Dental crown prices by material

Crown typeAverageReported range
Porcelain$1,399$915–$3,254
Porcelain fused to metal$1,114$770–$2,454
Metallic$1,211$821–$2,861
Resin / temporary$697$488–$1,593

Humana also reports material-specific examples for Orlando, Florida. Those local examples support keeping exact material labels visible, but they are not U.S.-wide benchmarks.

What may be separate in a crown quote?

A build-up or foundation, exam, imaging or another service may appear as a separate line. Current verified evidence does not support a universal build-up price, so the calculator accepts only the amount written on the user's estimate.

What changes a dental crown quote?

The current evidence supports keeping crown material/type and quote scope visible. Provider/location and separately charged services may also affect the total, but the site does not apply a hidden adjustment formula for those factors.

Crown repair and recementing are different price categories

CareCredit's general procedure list reports $765 average for broken crown repair and $126 average for recementing a dislodged crown. Those are separate services and must not be relabeled as the cost of a new or replacement crown.

How insurance can affect the patient amount

Coverage depends on the exact plan and service. Deductibles, allowed amounts, coinsurance, annual maximums, waiting periods, exclusions and network rules may affect what a patient owes. The calculator subtracts only a same-quote insurer estimate entered by the user.

Crown cost after a root canal or dental implant

Standalone crown pricing belongs on this page. If a crown is separately quoted after root-canal treatment, the root-canal treatment fee belongs on the root canal cost page. Implant-supported crown pricing belongs within the dental implant cost quote structure.
