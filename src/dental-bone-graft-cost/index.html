Dental bone graft costs

Dental bone graft cost by graft type and quote scope

Dental bone graft prices vary by graft material and procedure scope. The safest comparison is to keep each graft category separate and check whether it is included in an implant or extraction quote.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 16, 2026

How much does a dental bone graft cost?

CareCredit’s 2023–2024 national research reports different average-cost ranges by graft type. Because these procedures are not interchangeable, this page keeps the categories separate instead of presenting one blended “typical” price.

Graft typePublished range
Allograft$652–$1,575
Alloplast$576–$1,375
Autograft$2,161–$5,148
Xenograft$549–$1,386

CareCredit describes these as average costs per graft, based on Synchrony procedural-cost studies conducted across all 50 states plus Washington, D.C. They are published reference ranges, not provider quotes.

Bone graft prices depend on the graft type and quoted site

A graft can be quoted by the specific surgical site or procedure. The amount can also be affected by the material used and whether related services are billed separately. This page does not collapse the four CareCredit ranges into a single universal price because doing so would hide important scope differences.

Autograft, allograft, xenograft and alloplast are different quote categories

Dental bone grafts may use the patient’s own bone, human donor material, animal-derived material or synthetic substitutes. AAP guidance on ridge modification describes bone or bone substitutes as part of rebuilding a deficient ridge, but the choice of material and whether grafting is needed are clinical decisions for a dental professional.

Is the bone graft included in an implant or extraction quote?

Do not assume that an implant or extraction price includes grafting. The implant evidence used on this site treats bone grafting as a potentially separate quote component. If a provider includes it in a package, the written quote should say so; otherwise mark the inclusion as unknown until confirmed.

What can change the bone graft quote?

Price differences can reflect graft material, surgical site, procedure type, provider/location and whether membranes or related services are separately billed. A local clinic fee should not be treated as a U.S.-wide estimate.

How insurance can affect the patient cost

Coverage is plan-specific. A deductible, coinsurance, waiting period, annual or lifetime maximum, exclusion, network rule or allowed amount can affect the patient share. This page does not assume a fixed insurance percentage for bone grafting.

What this page does not price or decide

This page does not determine whether you need grafting, which graft material is appropriate, or whether sinus augmentation is indicated. Sinus-lift pricing is intentionally excluded from this page. Those questions require a clinical evaluation.
