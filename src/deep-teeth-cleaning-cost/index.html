How much does deep teeth cleaning cost?

For one quoted quadrant, the current published benchmark used on this page is $180–$295 without dental benefits. Delta Dental publishes that range for scaling and root planing. Its consumer page does not disclose the exact static-range data date or sample methodology, so treat the figure as a publisher-reported benchmark rather than a personalized quote.

For context, Humana gives a local example of $235–$303 per quadrant in Orlando, Florida, while ADA MouthHealthy is used here for the clinical definition of scaling and root planing rather than for a price benchmark. Keep the Humana figure local, do not merge the two price observations into a synthetic national range, and do not automatically multiply either observation by four.

What does “per quadrant” mean in a deep cleaning quote?

A quadrant is one-fourth of the mouth. A provider may quote scaling and root planing for one or more quadrants, but this page does not infer that number from symptoms, price or a published average.

The calculator calculates an average per quoted quadrant only after you enter and confirm the quadrant count from the written estimate.

What can be included or charged separately?

ADA MouthHealthy describes scaling and root planing as a deep cleaning below the gumline and notes that it may take more than one visit and may involve local anesthetic. That does not establish what a specific provider's price includes.

Humana lists factors such as the teeth treated, location, provider, X-rays and anesthesia when discussing cost. Its price statement does not establish that X-rays or anesthesia are included. Record these items from the written estimate rather than assuming a standard package.

What changes a scaling and root planing quote?

The current evidence supports keeping the quoted treatment extent visible. Humana also identifies factors such as the treated teeth, location and provider. The available sources do not support a universal formula for adjusting a quote by those factors.

Routine cleaning, deep cleaning, debridement and maintenance are not the same

This page owns scaling-and-root-planing pricing. Standard routine cleaning belongs on the dental cleaning cost page, and ongoing periodontal-maintenance pricing belongs on its own page. Do not treat gross debridement, laser procedures or unspecified “gum cleaning” labels as interchangeable with SRP unless the written quote clearly identifies the service.

These distinctions are for quote comparison only. This page does not determine which service a reader needs or what follow-up care is appropriate.

How insurance can affect the patient amount

Plan rules vary. Deductibles, coinsurance, network rules, frequency limits, annual maximums and the exact covered service may affect the patient amount under a particular policy, but this page does not assume any of those values.

The calculator subtracts only a user-entered insurer estimate confirmed to apply to the same written quote.
