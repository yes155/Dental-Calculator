What the current U.S. price data actually measures

The published $5,108 average and $1,800–$8,100 range are for clear aligners. Invisalign is a brand name within that broader treatment category. This page answers Invisalign cost intent but does not relabel category-level research as brand-only evidence.

What should a clear-aligner quote identify?

Confirm the treatment label, total quoted amount, whether retainers are included or separate, and which services the quote actually covers. The calculator does not add an unlisted retainer or any other missing service automatically.

Are retainers included in the aligner price?

Not necessarily. Retainer inclusion depends on the written quote or package. If a retainer is not listed or you are not sure, the calculator keeps that uncertainty visible instead of assuming a $0 charge.

What can change a clear-aligner quote?

The published source notes that actual costs vary by provider and geography. Treatment complexity or duration may also be reflected in a provider’s quote, but this page does not score clinical complexity or decide whether clear aligners are appropriate for you.

How insurance can affect what you pay

Orthodontic benefits are plan-specific. There is no universal coverage percentage, lifetime maximum, age rule or deductible built into this calculator. Enter an insurer estimate only when it covers the same scope as the written quote.

Invisalign and clear-aligner cost versus braces cost

Fixed braces are a separate treatment category with type-specific price evidence. See the braces cost guide and calculator rather than combining aligner and braces prices into one range.
