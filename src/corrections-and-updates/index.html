Trust & methodology

Corrections & Updates

Dental-cost evidence changes. We correct material errors in source files, preserve the revised scope and re-run the affected QA rather than making an undocumented display-only fix.

What can trigger a correction

  • a factual or clinical statement is unsupported or inaccurate;
  • a price is assigned to the wrong procedure, unit, geography or inclusion scope;
  • an insurance statement overstates coverage;
  • a calculator formula, assumption, label or edge case is misleading;
  • a source changes materially or is no longer appropriate.

How corrections are handled

We update the authoritative source content or calculator specification, test the affected system and review related pages that could share the same evidence or component. Material corrections should record what changed and the effective review date in the project change record.

Freshness

Price and insurance content is assigned a freshness schedule, commonly quarterly for high-change cost pages, with event-driven review when a material source changes. A displayed evidence-check date is not a promise that a price is still available from any particular provider.

Report an issue

Send a correction request to f.abdullah79@gmail.com. Include the page URL, the statement or calculator behavior in question, and any supporting source if available.
