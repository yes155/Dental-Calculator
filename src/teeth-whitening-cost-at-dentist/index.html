Teeth whitening cost at dentist

Current CareCredit/Synchrony U.S. research reports dental whitening by laser at $792 average ($611–$1,368) and Zoom! whitening at $583 average ($463–$1,011). These are separate professional methods, not one combined whitening price range.

What should a whitening quote identify?

Confirm the professional method, quoted price, number of office sessions if stated, and whether any consultation, exam, cleaning or take-home component is included or separately priced.

Is an exam or cleaning included in the whitening price?

Do not assume so. The published whitening method rows are procedure references, and a dental office may package related services differently. Compare the written inclusions before comparing prices.

What can change a professional whitening quote?

Method, provider, geography and the services included in the quote can affect the amount. This cost page does not decide whether whitening is suitable for a reader or how often it should be performed.

How insurance can affect whitening cost

Benefits are plan-specific. This page does not assume that professional whitening is covered or excluded and does not apply a universal insurance percentage. Verify the plan’s current terms if you expect a benefit.

Dentist whitening versus other cosmetic dental costs

Whitening, dental bonding and veneers have different procedure definitions and priced units. Compare bonding and veneers on their own cost pages.
