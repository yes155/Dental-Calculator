How much do dental veneers cost?

The current national evidence is most useful when the veneer material and priced unit match the written quote. Do not multiply a published per-veneer benchmark into a personal total; use your provider’s own quoted unit and amount.

What should a veneer quote identify?

Confirm the material or veneer type, whether the price is per veneer or a total, the number of veneers when relevant, and what the written amount says is included.

What may be included or charged separately?

The source table states that consultation and potential maintenance or repair are not included in those published type rows. A provider quote can use different packaging, so the calculator asks whether other charges are included, separate, not listed or unclear rather than assuming $0.

What can change a veneer quote?

Material, quoted unit, number of veneers, provider and geography can affect a written price. This page does not decide whether veneers are appropriate or which material should be used.

How insurance can affect what you pay

Benefits are plan-specific. The calculator has no default cosmetic coverage percentage, deductible or annual maximum. Enter an insurer estimate only when it covers the same scope as the written quote.

Veneers cost versus dental bonding and whitening

Bonding is a separate per-tooth cost topic, while professional whitening is priced by whitening method. Compare them on their own pages rather than mixing the benchmarks.
