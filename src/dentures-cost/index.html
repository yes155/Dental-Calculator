How much do dentures cost?

The useful answer depends on the denture category and priced unit. The source's broad $340–$11,018 endpoints span low-cost removable and premium removable categories; they should not be read as one like-for-like denture quote range.

Why $340–$11,018 is not one like-for-like denture range

Those endpoints come from different denture categories: low-cost removable at one end and premium removable at the other. They describe market breadth, not the range for one standardized denture service.

What should a denture quote identify?

Confirm whether the quote is for a complete or partial denture, the material/design label, the arch or other unit covered, whether an implant-supported amount is per implant or a total, and what services the office says are included.

What may be included or charged separately?

Consultation, extractions, imaging, relines, repairs, temporary prostheses, implants and other services can be separate depending on the written treatment estimate. This calculator does not add or zero out anything the quote leaves unclear.

How insurance can affect what you pay

Denture benefits vary by plan. The calculator uses no universal percentage, deductible, annual maximum or replacement-frequency assumption; enter only a same-scope insurer estimate you actually have.

Dentures cost versus dental bridges and implants

A removable denture, fixed bridge and dental implant use different units and service scopes. See dental bridge cost and dental implant cost for their separate evidence.
