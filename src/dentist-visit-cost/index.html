Dentist visit cost

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 17, 2026

How much does a dentist visit cost?

The best current national evidence in this review is for a bundled routine visit rather than an exam-only fee. CareCredit/Synchrony reports the bundled benchmark from 2023–2024 ASQ360° research covering all 50 states and Washington, D.C.

Published bundle: $203 average and $50–$350 for a routine dental exam that includes a full cleaning and X-rays. Use that as broad bundle context, not as an exam-only cash price, dentist quote or insurer allowed amount.

Why the current national price is a bundled visit, not an exam-only fee

The source explicitly includes three components in the priced category: the routine exam, a full dental cleaning and X-rays. Removing the cleaning and imaging in order to call the same number an “exam price” would change the priced unit and overstate what the evidence supports.

If your written estimate lists only an examination or evaluation, compare that service line with another exam-only quote rather than assuming the bundled national figure applies.

What kind of dental visit is on the quote?

Dental visit labels are not interchangeable. ADA terminology and current plan documents distinguish multiple evaluation and visit categories. The label on the written estimate matters because a periodic evaluation, comprehensive evaluation, problem-focused evaluation, consultation or after-hours office visit can represent a different billable service.

Periodic and comprehensive evaluations

ADA guidance distinguishes periodic oral evaluation from comprehensive oral evaluation. This page can help identify the wording on a quote, but it does not determine which evaluation a particular person needs.

Problem-focused, consultation and after-hours visits

Current plan documents also separate limited/problem-focused evaluations, consultations and after-hours office visits from routine periodic or comprehensive evaluations. Do not assign the $203 bundled routine-visit benchmark to one of these categories unless the written quote actually has the same included scope.

What should a dentist-visit quote identify?

  • Service or evaluation label: periodic, comprehensive, problem-focused, consultation or another named category.
  • Cleaning: included, separately billed, not listed or unclear.
  • X-rays: included, separately billed, not listed or unclear; image-specific pricing belongs on the dental X-ray guide.
  • Price type: provider charge, bundled cash quote, insurer allowed amount or plan member cost.
  • Other visit charges: any separate consultation, after-hours or office-visit line.

Are cleaning and X-rays included in a dental checkup price?

Not universally. The CareCredit benchmark is a specific bundle that includes both a full cleaning and X-rays. Other written quotes may list an exam by itself or show cleaning and imaging as separate lines.

For cleaning-only prices, use the dental cleaning cost guide. For imaging-only prices, use the dental X-ray cost guide. Do not treat a missing line as a zero-dollar service; confirm what the dental office means by the quoted total.

What can change a dentist-visit quote?

Supported differences include the visit category, whether cleaning or imaging is bundled, provider and geographic pricing, network status and plan-specific cost sharing. The current evidence does not support a universal multiplier for any of those factors.

Compare like-for-like written service descriptions rather than using one generic “dentist visit” number for unlike appointment types.

How insurance can affect what you pay for a dental visit

There is no universal insurance percentage, copay or number of covered exams that applies to every plan. Current plan documents can classify periodic, comprehensive, problem-focused and consultation services differently and apply their own frequency, network and member-cost rules.

For the broader mechanics of deductibles, allowed amounts, network status and annual maximums, see dental insurance and out-of-pocket costs. Verify the exact visit category and benefits with the dental office and insurer or plan administrator.
