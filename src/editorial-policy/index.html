Trust & methodology

Editorial Policy

Our editorial process is designed for cost education: answer the reader’s financial question, preserve the evidence scope, state uncertainty and keep clinical decisions with qualified dental professionals.

Source hierarchy

We prefer U.S. government and public-health sources, professional dental organizations, peer-reviewed research, universities and dental schools, major insurers and recognized cost datasets. Specialist sources can add bounded context. A single dental practice may be used only as a clearly labeled local example, never as a U.S.-wide benchmark.

How we write cost claims

Important price figures should be traceable to a procedure, geography, date or research vintage, priced unit, price type, assumptions and source. We do not combine unlike source ranges merely to create a wider or more attractive national range. If a source prices a bundle, the published copy keeps that bundle intact.

Clinical and insurance boundaries

Clinical claims require appropriate evidence. The site does not diagnose, determine treatment need or eligibility, select a dental material for a reader or guarantee coverage. Insurance language accounts for plan-specific deductibles, networks, cost sharing, annual maximums, exclusions, frequency limits and other terms rather than assuming one universal percentage.

Author and reviewer attribution

Farrukh Abdullah is the site’s Researcher & Writer. Juliana Maia Teixeira serves as Clinical & Scientific Reviewer. Reviewer credentials may be shown on the reviewer profile, but an individual article is labeled Reviewed by Juliana Maia Teixeira only after that exact article version has actually been reviewed. A site-level reviewer relationship is not treated as blanket review of every page.

Corrections and freshness

Price and insurance pages are reviewed on their assigned freshness schedule and when a material source changes. Significant factual, price-scope or calculator errors should be corrected in source files and recorded through the site’s correction workflow.

Commercial influence

Being cited as a source does not make a company or organization an endorser of Dental Cost Calculator. Any future paid, sponsored or affiliate relationship that could affect a page will be disclosed where relevant. Editorial evidence rules do not change because of a commercial relationship.
