How much does a root canal cost?

CareCredit reports a national average of $1,165 and a broad reported range of $500–$1,800 for root-canal treatment based on 2023–2024 Synchrony/ASQ360° research across the 50 states and District of Columbia.

The same source reports separate ranges by tooth type, so a written quote is easier to compare when the tooth category is stated. The benchmark does not establish that a final crown, filling, build-up, post, exam or imaging is included.

Is the crown or final filling included in a root canal quote?

Do not assume it is included. ADA MouthHealthy and the American Association of Endodontists describe a definitive restoration after root-canal treatment, which may be a filling or crown. That clinical sequence does not prove that the restoration is bundled into a particular price.

Humana's Orlando examples make the boundary especially clear: anterior $900, premolar $1,017 and molar $1,175 are explicitly labeled as root-canal prices excluding final restoration. Those figures are local examples, not national benchmarks.

What can change a root canal quote?

The current evidence supports keeping the tooth category and written component scope visible. A dentist or endodontist may provide treatment, but this page does not apply a universal specialist premium or decide which provider a reader needs.

Check whether the quote separately lists the final crown or filling, build-up/post, imaging, exam or another charge. If the written estimate is unclear, keep that item marked “Not sure” rather than guessing.

What if the quote says retreatment?

The American Association of Endodontists describes retreatment as a distinct later endodontic procedure for a previously treated tooth. The current verified source set does not provide a defensible national retreatment price.

CALC-005 can total a retreatment quote you enter, but it does not generate or compare against a retreatment benchmark.

How insurance can affect the patient amount

Coverage depends on the exact plan and service scope. Deductibles, allowed amounts, coinsurance, network rules, waiting periods, exclusions and annual maximums may affect the final patient responsibility. This page does not assume those values.

The calculator subtracts only an insurer estimate that you enter and confirm applies to the same written quote.

Root canal cost versus crown cost

DEN-005 owns the root-canal treatment fee and combined quote organization. Standalone crown pricing belongs on the dental crown cost page. Filling pricing belongs on the dental filling cost page. Keeping those owners separate prevents a crown or filling benchmark from being silently merged into the root-canal benchmark.
