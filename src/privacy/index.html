Trust & methodology

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current prelaunch site. It must remain consistent with the actual production scripts, hosting, analytics, advertising and forms in use.

Calculator inputs

Dental Calculator’s current calculator design is intended to process quote amounts and related selections in the browser. Quote values are not intentionally sent to, stored by or serialized to an external service by the calculator logic. Calculator inputs should not include patient names, insurance-member identifiers, account numbers or other sensitive personal information.

Email contact

If you contact us by email, we receive the address you use and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to the message, investigate a correction or address the editorial or technical issue described.

Hosting and request data

The site is delivered through web-hosting and network infrastructure. Those providers may process standard request and security data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps and diagnostic information as part of operating and protecting the service.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current prelaunch source does not intentionally rely on advertising or analytics cookies. If production deployment adds analytics, advertising, consent tooling or other tracking technology, this policy and the deployed scripts must be reviewed together before launch so the disclosure matches the site’s actual behavior.

Third-party links

Pages may link to source organizations, insurers, professional profiles and other external websites. When you follow an external link, the destination site operates under its own privacy practices.

Data minimization

Do not send passwords, full insurance-member IDs, bank or card numbers, Social Security numbers, complete medical records or other sensitive information that is not necessary for a correction or editorial question.

Questions

Privacy questions can be sent through the Contact page. Material changes to this policy should be reflected in the site’s Corrections & Updates record.
