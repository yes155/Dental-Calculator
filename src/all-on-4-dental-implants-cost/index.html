All-on-4 dental implant cost: per-arch quote guide

CareCredit reports a U.S. national average of $15,176 and a reported range of $11,640–$27,500 for All-on-4 dental implants, based on a 2024 study across all 50 states plus Washington, D.C. The article describes treatment of either the upper or lower jaw, but the price sentence itself does not independently label the figure “per arch,” so your own quote’s arch count must be confirmed.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 16, 2026

How much do All-on-4 dental implants cost?

CareCredit reports a $15,176 national average and a $11,640–$27,500 range for All-on-4 dental implants. The underlying 2024 Synchrony Average Procedural Cost Study was conducted by ASQ360° across all 50 states plus Washington, D.C.

The reference is a published procedure benchmark, not a provider quote. The cost block does not establish that extraction, bone grafting, imaging, a temporary bridge, a final bridge and follow-up visits are all included.

What does All-on-4 mean in a dental quote?

All-on-4 is a Nobel Biocare trademark and full-arch treatment concept involving a bridge supported by four implants. Manufacturer material is used here only for naming and concept attribution.

This page does not use manufacturer material to claim clinical superiority, universal graft-free treatment, expected success rates or patient suitability.

Is an All-on-4 price per arch or for a full mouth?

The CareCredit article describes treatment of either the upper or lower jaw, but the price sentence itself does not independently state that the published amount is a confirmed per-arch fee. A written provider quote should therefore state whether it covers the upper arch, lower arch, or both arches.

If that scope is unclear, do not infer it from the dollar amount. The calculator below requires the arch count to be confirmed from the written quote before it displays a normalized per-arch figure.

What may be included in an All-on-4 package?

A provider quote may include or separately list implant placement, abutments, a temporary/provisional bridge, a final bridge or prosthesis, extractions, bone grafting, imaging/exam, sedation/anesthesia, follow-up visits and other components.

The published CareCredit price block does not establish that all of those items are bundled. For comparison purposes, each component should be recorded as included, separately quoted, not on the quote, or unknown.

What changes an All-on-4 quote?

Quote totals can differ because of arch count, provider/location, materials, whether additional implants are quoted, and the package scope. Separately quoted extraction, grafting, imaging, sedation or prosthetic stages can also change the entered total.

Those variables should come from the written estimate. This page does not infer treatment steps or package contents from the price alone.

How insurance can affect out-of-pocket cost

Implant coverage varies by plan. Deductibles, waiting periods, exclusions, annual or lifetime maximums, network/allowed-amount rules and the exact services on the treatment plan can affect patient responsibility.

The calculator does not assume a coverage percentage. Verify plan-specific benefits with the insurer or plan administrator.
