How much do All-on-4 dental implants cost?

A current published U.S. benchmark is a $15,176 national average with a $11,640–$27,500 range for All-on-4 dental implants. CareCredit reports those figures from the 2024 Synchrony Average Procedural Cost Study conducted by ASQ360° across all 50 states plus Washington, D.C.

For how published dental price benchmarks are selected and scoped, see our cost data methodology.

The reference is a published procedure benchmark, not a provider quote. The cost block does not establish that tooth removal, bone grafting, imaging, a temporary bridge, a final bridge and follow-up visits are all included.

What does All-on-4 mean in a dental quote?

All-on-4 is a Nobel Biocare trademark and full-arch treatment concept involving a bridge supported by four implants. Manufacturer material is used here only for naming and concept attribution.

This page does not use manufacturer material to claim clinical superiority, universal graft-free treatment, expected success rates or patient suitability.

Is an All-on-4 price per arch or for a full mouth?

The CareCredit article describes treatment of either the upper or lower jaw, but the price sentence itself does not independently state that the published amount is a confirmed per-arch fee. A written provider quote should therefore state whether it covers the upper arch, lower arch, or both arches.

If that is unclear, do not infer it from the dollar amount. The calculator requires the arch count to be confirmed from the written quote before it displays a per-arch figure.

What may be included in an All-on-4 package?

A provider quote may include or separately list implant placement, connectors (abutments), temporary teeth or bridge, final teeth or bridge, tooth removal, bone grafting, X-rays or scans, sedation, follow-up visits and other charges.

The published CareCredit price block does not establish that all of those items are bundled. For comparison purposes, each item should be recorded as included, a separate charge, not listed, or not sure.

What changes an All-on-4 quote?

Quote totals can differ because of arch count, provider/location, materials, whether additional implants are quoted, and what the package includes. Separately charged tooth removal, grafting, imaging, sedation or prosthetic stages can also change the entered total.

Those variables should come from the written estimate. This page does not infer treatment steps or package contents from the price alone.

How insurance can affect out-of-pocket cost

Implant coverage varies by plan. Deductibles, waiting periods, exclusions, annual or lifetime maximums, network/allowed-amount rules and the exact services on the treatment plan can affect patient responsibility.

For deductibles, coinsurance, annual maximums and other plan-specific factors, see dental insurance and out-of-pocket costs.

The calculator does not assume a coverage percentage. Verify plan-specific benefits with the insurer or plan administrator.

All-on-4 vs. generic full-arch implant quotes

All-on-4-specific evidence should not be substituted for every full-arch implant treatment. Generic one-arch and two-arch quote comparison belongs on the full-mouth dental implant page.
