Dental implant cost calculator: single-tooth quote components

A dental implant quote can cover the implant body alone or a wider treatment package. This page separates the amounts and inclusions so you can compare a written quote without assuming that every fee is bundled.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 16, 2026

How much does a dental implant cost?

CareCredit reports a national average of $2,143 and a reported range of $1,646–$4,157 for the dental implant process/artificial root. Its 2023–2024 research covers all 50 states plus Washington, D.C. The source explicitly says the crown is not included; state figures also exclude tooth extraction and some office-related fees.

That makes this a useful reference for the implant portion of treatment, but it is not an all-inclusive single-tooth replacement price. Your own written quote may list the implant body, abutment, crown, extraction, grafting, imaging or other services separately.

What does a single-tooth implant quote include?

The FDA describes a dental implant system as an implant body placed in the jaw, an abutment attached to the implant body, and an artificial tooth or prosthetic restoration above it. Those components can appear as separate line items in a dental quote.

Quote componentWhat to verify
Implant body / processWhether the amount is for the artificial root and placement only
AbutmentWhether included with the implant or billed separately
Final crown / prosthesisWhether included; the CareCredit implant benchmark above excludes the crown
Extraction, graft, imaging or sedationWhether included, separate, absent from the quote, or still unknown

Implant post, abutment and crown costs are different quote components

A figure labeled “implant cost” does not always mean a complete restored tooth. For this reason, this site does not add an estimated crown or abutment price automatically to the CareCredit implant benchmark. The safest comparison is to keep each provider’s written scope visible.

What changes the price of a dental implant quote?

Published prices vary by location, provider and what the quoted package includes. CareCredit also reports a separate average of $3,255 for an immediate-load implant category, but that number is kept separate from the standard implant benchmark rather than blended into one synthetic range.

Materials can also be described differently. FDA guidance notes that implant systems may use titanium or zirconium oxide (zirconia). A zirconia implant body is not the same thing as a zirconia crown, so a material label should always be tied to the component it describes.

How insurance can change out-of-pocket cost

Dental implant coverage varies by plan. Deductibles, annual or lifetime maximums, waiting periods, exclusions and allowed-amount rules can affect the final patient cost. This calculator therefore accepts only an insurer estimate that you already have for the same quote scope; it does not assume that insurance pays a fixed percentage.

Mini, zirconia and immediate-load implant quote labels

Different labels may describe different implant systems, materials or timing. The current evidence set supports a separate CareCredit immediate-load benchmark, but it does not support national default prices for mini implants or zirconia implant bodies. Those categories are not filled in by the calculator.

When extraction or bone graft fees are separate

Extraction and bone grafting may be separate quote components. Marking an item “not on quote” means only that it is not listed in the written quote; it does not mean the service is clinically unnecessary.
