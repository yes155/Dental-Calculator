Dental retainer cost

Current U.S. CareCredit/Synchrony cost data reports average prices of $344 for a bonded retainer, $181 for a clear plastic retainer and $302 for a molded acrylic retainer. These are separate retainer categories, not one universal retainer price, and replacement fees can be set by the orthodontist.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 17, 2026

How much does a dental retainer cost?

The clearest current national evidence is type-specific rather than one all-retainer average. The published values below are reference averages, not guaranteed replacement prices or a recommendation for any retainer type.

Retainer cost by type

Retainer categoryPublished average
Bonded retainer$344
Clear plastic retainer$181
Molded acrylic retainer$302

Source: CareCredit/Synchrony U.S. dental procedure cost data. The three rows should not be averaged into a site-created “typical retainer price.”

Is a retainer included in braces or clear-aligner treatment?

It depends on the written orthodontic quote or package. Dental Calculator does not assume that a retainer is included and does not assign $0 when the quote is silent. The braces and clear-aligner calculators therefore ask whether the retainer is included, separate, not listed or unclear.

How much does a replacement retainer cost?

The American Association of Orthodontists states that the fee for a replacement retainer is determined by the orthodontist. The national averages above should therefore not be presented as guaranteed replacement-retainer fees.

What should a retainer quote identify?

Confirm the retainer type, whether the price is for one device or another stated unit, whether the fee is for an initial or replacement retainer, and any services the office says are included. This page does not choose a retainer type or prescribe a wear schedule.

How insurance can affect retainer cost

Coverage is plan-specific. Do not assume a universal orthodontic percentage, lifetime maximum or replacement benefit. Verify the exact retainer benefit and remaining plan limits with the insurer or plan administrator.
