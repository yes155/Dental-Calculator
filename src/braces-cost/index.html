How much do braces cost?

The most useful current national references differ by fixed-brace type. Keep the metal, ceramic and lingual rows separate when comparing a written quote; combining their endpoints would create a range the source never published.

Braces cost by type

Fixed-brace typePublished averagePublished range
Metal braces$6,343$2,500–$10,000
Ceramic braces$5,834$4,480–$11,312
Lingual braces$9,221$7,321–$17,411

Source: CareCredit/Synchrony U.S. cost research. The rows are separate categories; there is no site-created all-types braces range.

What should a braces quote identify?

Confirm the fixed-brace type, total quoted amount, retainer scope, accessory or visit inclusions if stated, and the exact services covered by any insurance estimate. The calculator does not add accessory charges that are not written in the quote.

Are retainers and accessory charges included?

Not universally. A written orthodontic package may include some items and charge others separately. Retainer inclusion is therefore an explicit calculator input rather than an automatic assumption.

What can change a braces quote?

Provider and geographic pricing can vary, and treatment details can change a provider’s written fee. This site does not use brace type or any clinical description to decide treatment suitability or automatically alter the amount you entered.

How insurance can affect what you pay

Orthodontic benefits can use plan-specific rules and limits. This calculator has no universal percentage, lifetime maximum, age rule or network assumption; it uses only a same-scope insurance payment estimate that you enter.

Braces cost versus clear-aligner cost

Clear aligners are removable tray-like appliances and have their own national cost evidence. See the Invisalign and clear-aligner cost calculator rather than mixing clear-aligner prices into a fixed-braces range.
