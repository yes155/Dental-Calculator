Cleaning and gum-care costs

Periodontal maintenance cost

Delta Dental reports $140–$220 without dental benefits for periodontal maintenance cleaning and states that the price covers a whole-mouth cleaning. This is an ongoing maintenance visit after prior periodontal treatment, not the initial scaling and root planing priced on the deep-cleaning page.

Research and written by Farrukh Abdullah

Evidence checked September 17, 2026

How much does periodontal maintenance cost?

The approved evidence for this page is Delta Dental's $140–$220 range without dental benefits for one whole-mouth periodontal maintenance cleaning. The source does not disclose enough methodology to label that range as a 2026 national fee study, so this page preserves it as a publisher-reported reference rather than a guaranteed provider price.

What does the whole-mouth price cover?

The source describes the unit as a whole-mouth maintenance cleaning for a patient who has had previous periodontal treatment such as deep cleaning or gum surgery. The price does not establish that every exam, X-ray or other service is included in every provider's visit fee.

Periodontal maintenance is different from deep cleaning

Initial scaling and root planing has its own cost owner and pricing unit. See deep teeth cleaning cost for that treatment. Routine prophylaxis is also separate; see dental cleaning cost.

What can change a maintenance visit quote?

A provider's fee, location, services included in the visit and plan terms can affect the amount billed or paid. This page does not diagnose periodontal disease or decide whether maintenance is indicated.

How insurance frequency limits can affect what you pay

Dental benefits can use plan-specific frequency rules, networks, deductibles, cost sharing and annual limits. This page does not assume a universal maintenance frequency or coverage percentage. Verify the exact plan terms and remaining benefits.

Why this page does not assume a yearly visit schedule

A per-visit price cannot be converted into an annual total without a visit count. Dental Calculator does not prescribe or assume a periodontal-maintenance interval. If a dentist has given you a schedule, multiply only the actual provider quote by that documented number of visits for personal budgeting.
